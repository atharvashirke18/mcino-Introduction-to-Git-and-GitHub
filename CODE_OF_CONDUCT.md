# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct
that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not comply
with this Code of Conduct and will take appropriate, fair corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review and investigate
reports promptly and fairly while respecting the reporter's privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of each violation and may take
the following actions:

1. **Correction:** A private or public explanation and warning about the
   inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning and restrictions on interactions if needed.
3. **Temporary ban:** A temporary suspension from project spaces for serious
   or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent ban:** Removal from project spaces for a sustained pattern of
   violations or severe misconduct.
